WHAT THIS APP CAN PROVE

Scope and guardrails

Use the tool as structured evidence collection, followed by human review and server-side remediation.

Built in

  • Read-only GET, HEAD, and OPTIONS requests
  • Same-origin redirects only
  • 8-second timeout and 256 KB response cap
  • Bounded request counts and cancellation
  • Tokens and cookies excluded from reports

Not built in

  • No endpoint brute force or unrestricted crawler
  • No password attack or credential spraying
  • No XSS payload execution
  • No POST, PUT, PATCH, or DELETE request
  • No claim that a site is vulnerability-free
Interpretation matters. A similar 2xx response can indicate broken authorization, but it can also be a public or intentionally shared resource. Verify policy and business context before reporting a vulnerability.
